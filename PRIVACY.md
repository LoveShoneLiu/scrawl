# Privacy Policy for Scrawl

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026  
**Developer:** Shaofei Liu  
**App:** Scrawl (`com.shaofeiliu.scribblepond`)

This policy describes how Scrawl handles information. Scrawl is a drawing toy for young children. It works on the device. It does not require an account.

## Summary

Scrawl does **not** collect personal data.  
It does **not** send drawings, names, or device identifiers to us or to any third party.  
It does **not** include ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

## What the app stores on the device

If a parent saves a pond, Scrawl stores on **this iPad only**:

- drawings the child put into the pond
- a small thumbnail of a saved pond
- the time a pond was saved
- whether sound is on or off

This stays in the app’s private storage on the device. It is not uploaded. Deleting the app, or deleting a saved pond in the app, removes that local data.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect:

- name, email, phone number, or address
- photos from the camera or photo library
- location
- contacts
- identifiers for advertising
- analytics or crash reports
- payment information

The app does not sign in to Apple, Google, or any other service.

## Children

Scrawl is made for young children, including children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data and does not communicate with our servers, we do not receive children’s information.

Parents can save or delete ponds, turn sound off, and empty the pond from parent settings in the app.

## Third parties

Scrawl does not share data with third parties. It does not embed advertising, analytics, or social SDKs.

## Internet

Scrawl does not need a network connection to draw or play. It does not load web pages inside the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and this file in the project repository.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at  
https://github.com/LoveShoneLiu/scrawl/issues

# Scrawl 隐私政策（中文）

**更新日期：** 2026 年 9 月 2 日  
**开发者：** Shaofei Liu  
**App：** Scrawl（`com.shaofeiliu.scribblepond`）

Scrawl 是给幼儿用的涂鸦玩具，在设备上本地运行，不需要账号。

## 摘要

Scrawl **不收集**个人数据。  
不会把画作、姓名或设备标识发给开发者或任何第三方。  
没有广告、统计分析、追踪或 App 内购买。

## 本机存储

如果家长保存了池塘，App **只在这台 iPad 上**保存：

- 孩子放进池塘的涂鸦
- 已保存池塘的缩略图
- 保存时间
- 声音开关

数据不会上传。删除 App，或在 App 里删除已保存的池塘，就会去掉这些本机数据。

## 我们不收集的内容

不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地址、相册、位置、通讯录、广告标识符、分析或崩溃报告、支付信息。也不登录任何第三方账号。

## 儿童

本 App 面向幼儿，包括 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。App 不采集数据、不连接我们的服务器，因此我们也收不到儿童信息。家长可在家长设置里保存或删除池塘、关闭声音、清空池塘。

## 第三方与网络

没有向第三方分享数据，没有广告/统计/社交 SDK。涂鸦和玩耍不需要联网，App 内也不能打开网页。

## 联系

隐私相关问题请在 GitHub 提 issue：  
https://github.com/LoveShoneLiu/scrawl/issues
